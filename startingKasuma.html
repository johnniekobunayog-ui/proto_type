KABANATA I
KASÚMA
ANG HANDAAN

It was 1998. The small village was alive with celebration. Colorful decorations filled the streets as families gathered for the annual town fiesta.

Elias had returned home just in time for the celebration. The smell of freshly prepared food filled the air, and laughter echoed throughout the village.

But beneath the music and festivities, something felt wrong. A few villagers refused to eat the food being served. Others whispered about strange incidents that had happened during previous celebrations.

An old woman quietly warned Elias about a local legend—a mysterious creature called the Yanggaw, said to hide among ordinary people.

Before leaving, she gave him one final warning: never accept food from someone you do not trust.

"Sa piyestahan, hindi lahat ng handa ay pagkain, kung minsan ay may nakatago. Mag-ingat ka sa pagkaing hindi mo kilala ang pinagmulan, at baka maging isa ka sa kanila."

PHILIPPINES — 1998
